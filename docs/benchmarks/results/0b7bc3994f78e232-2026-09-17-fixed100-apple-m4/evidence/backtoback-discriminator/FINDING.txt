iox2 FLOOR DISCRIMINATOR - backtoback probe (readme-ruled amendment 1, stage 1)
==============================================================================
Question it answers: the fixed100 run of this cell wrote `did_not_sustain` at
every ladder rung, and inverting its skip counter implied ~42-98 ms per round
trip. Two candidate causes: (a) the iceoryx2 round trip really is that slow on
macOS, or (b) the defect is in the PACED path (the bench's RateLimiter sleep),
and the transport is fine. backtoback runs the identical binary with NO rate
limiter, so it separates them.

VERDICT: (b). The transport is fine. The defect is the paced path.

c2bf1353305141f2, payload 64 B, rep 1, 10000 measured samples (+1000 warmup),
STOCK posture (CER_BENCH_DMA_LOCK=0), CI verified idle:

The raw iceoryx2 round trip on this machine is SUB-MICROSECOND - the fastest
cell in the whole campaign, and ~4x faster than the Cerulion mono leg (3.46 us)
and ~110x faster than the split leg (88 us), which is the shape you would
expect of a raw floor.

WHY THE fixed100 CELL STILL FAILED, restated with both numbers in hand: the
round trip costs ~0.8 us, so a 100 Hz grid slot (10 ms) is ~12,000 round trips
wide. The cell could not miss its slots because of transport work. What it
does between slots is `thread::sleep(remaining - 50 us)` then a spin to the
deadline (native/src/lib.rs:656-684). On macOS that sleep overshoots by tens of
milliseconds, so EVERY slot lands late, `next_grid_slot` jumps the schedule
index forward, and the skips accumulate past the slot count - which is exactly
the "skipped 6669 of 2100" shape the fixed100 log carries. The cost also GREW
with the rung period (~42 ms at 10 ms slots, ~80 ms at 20 ms, ~98 ms at 50 ms),
which is a sleep-granularity signature, not a payload or transport one.

CPU LINE: the fixed100 attempt held 99.6 % CPU for 6 min 56 s without finishing
one 64 B rung (spin-bound, sampled by ps). The backtoback probe finished the
same size in under 1 second - too fast for the 2 s CPU sampler to take a single
sample, which is itself the contrast: the CPU log for this probe is EMPTY
because there was nothing to sample.

SCOPE / WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY: the backtoback curve is NOT a fixed100 row and
must never be presented as one - it was not rate-paced at all, so it carries no
rate label and is not comparable to the 100 Hz rows in HEADLINE.md. What it
establishes is narrower and sufficient: the transport is sound on macOS, and the
fixed100 refusal is a PACING artefact. It does not measure this cell under the
campaign's own 100 Hz spec, because on this platform that spec cannot be met by
this binary - which is the finding, not a gap in it.
