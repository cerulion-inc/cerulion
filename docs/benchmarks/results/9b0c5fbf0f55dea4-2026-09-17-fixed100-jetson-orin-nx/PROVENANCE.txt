GOVERNOR - THE HEADLINE CAVEAT, RAISED BY THE SUITE ITSELF
  The suite printed, verbatim, at every sweep:
    "! cpu governor is 'schedutil', not 'performance' - DVFS can move small-payload
     p50s by double digits; set the performance governor before a citable sweep
     (recorded in run.json either way)"
  It was NOT acted on, for two independent reasons: the box rules forbid sudo, and
  STOCK is the commissioned posture - schedutil IS this Jetson's stock governor.
  The consequence is stated rather than smoothed over: the x86-64 Linux published
  row was measured under the PERFORMANCE governor and this arm64 row under
  schedutil, and the suite's own estimate of that gap is double-digit percent at
  SMALL payloads. run.json records the governor for every invocation.

ROS 2 CELLS ARE ABSENT, AND THE REASON IS MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED
  An arm64 Jazzy image DOES exist on this box (latency_bench:jazzy, arm64/linux,
  created 2026-08-16) carrying label
  com.cerulion.bench.repo_git_sha=5abb7c3d9f2607771ff328d41e5d1ee2afba3208.
  The established reuse rule is: reusable only when the label sha equals the run
  sha, OR benches/latency/ros2 is byte-identical between the two. NEITHER holds -
  5abb7c3d..ba5d75c5c touches 19 files, +4159/-262.
  It is worse than a stale label. bench.py bind-mounts run_bench.sh, configs/ and
  verify_shm.sh read-only FROM THE CHECKOUT, so the DRIVER would have been the
  current one (+2080 lines), while the 16 remaining changed files are BAKED INTO
  THE IMAGE and include EVERY measuring node - latency_node.cpp (+345),
  composed_rtt_node.cpp (+254), latency_node_rcl.cpp (+249), ping_node.cpp,
  pong_node.cpp, pong_node_rcl.cpp - plus three headers that did not exist at the
  label sha (stamp_codec.hpp, sample_gate.hpp, msg_class_dispatch.hpp). Running the
  new driver against those old binaries is a mismatched pair, not a reuse. Building
  an image was out of scope for this lane, so no ROS 2 row was produced.

run.json git_dirty = TRUE, AND HERE IS EXACTLY WHY
  The worktree carried ONE untracked path and no tracked change at all. Verbatim
  `git status --porcelain` in the measured worktree, complete:
      ?? target
  `git diff --stat HEAD` is EMPTY - zero tracked-file modifications - and HEAD is
  ba5d75c5c226e749c9f5e8846c55c21b8bfd1035. So the code that was built and measured
  is byte-identical to the requested sha.
  `target` is a SYMLINK to the shared box target, and it is there on purpose. The
  box rule is that per-lane worktrees share one warm box target under the box lock.
  It cannot be done with CARGO_TARGET_DIR: run_workspace.sh resolves the CLI as
  "${CERULION:-$REPO_ROOT/target/release/cerulion}", so exporting CARGO_TARGET_DIR
  puts the binary somewhere the runner does not look; and bench.py's
  refuse_foreign_cerulion_override rejects an exported CERULION= pointing outside
  the checkout. A symlink at $REPO_ROOT/target satisfies both. The workspace
  cdylibs keep their own in-worktree target, so run_workspace.sh's
  `rm -rf $SCRIPT_DIR/target/debug` never touches another lane's artifacts.
  The lane's TREE-MATCH assert therefore required HEAD == the sha AND zero dirty
  paths after excluding exactly this one untracked symlink.

WINDOW HYGIENE
  systemd timers were snapshotted at START and END and gated on. The campaign
  opened only after a hard gate confirmed no timer within ~25 min and no mandb
  process alive. mandb - the filesystem walk that produced a 138.7 s stall on the
  x86-64 box - was never alive and never inside the horizon (it was >3 h out).
  anacron.timer is HOURLY on this box, so a ~90 min campaign cannot avoid it. The
  per-rep gate therefore waits up to 720 s and then proceeds with a loud
  TIMER-GATE-DEGRADED line naming the timer, rather than deadlocking. That fired
  exactly once: rep 2 waited the full cap and ran across anacron's 02:32:23Z fire.
  IT IS NOT SWEPT UNDER THE RUG, AND IT IS NOT MERELY ASSERTED HARMLESS - it is
  checked. Per-rep median-of-sizes p50 (us):
    Cerulion split  rep1 27.79  rep2 28.59  rep3 27.62  rep4 26.59  rep5 28.49
    Cerulion mono   rep1 24.30  rep2 22.64  rep3 22.64  rep4 23.19  rep5 22.74
    raw iceoryx2    rep1  3.10  rep2  2.85  rep3  2.11  rep4  3.06  rep5  1.58
  Per-rep samples over 100 us (of 20000 per rep):
    Cerulion split  22 21 24 16 25      Cerulion mono  17 14 31 17 24
    raw iceoryx2     2  2  2  2  7
  Rep 2 sits inside the spread on every cell and carries the LOWEST mono p50 and
  the lowest or joint-lowest tail counts. The timer measurably did not perturb it.
