src/quaternion.rs  UnitQuaternion::slerp  replace < with <= in the
                   `theta < Self::SLERP_LERP_THRESHOLD` branch
    Equivalent in practice. The two forms differ only when theta is exactly
    the 1e-6 double, and no real input lands on that one value: theta comes
    from 2*atan_unit(|b-a|/|b+a|) of two unit quaternions, so a test would
    have to hand-craft an input pair whose ratio rounds to exactly the
    tan(5e-7) that gives that single point. Even there the two blends differ only by
    order theta cubed (below 1e-17 absolute). That is under an ulp of the
    components near 1, but not bit-exact on the small components, so the
    reason is the unreachable point, not an ulp bound.
