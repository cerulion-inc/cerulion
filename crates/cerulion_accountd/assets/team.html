Cerulion / Team & access
Honest scope. Revocation is online-enforced: a robot refuses a revoked account or device once it syncs the new access epoch — an offline robot keeps honouring a desk-carried grant until then. Grants themselves are carried by the desk and signed offline, so the account service cannot enumerate them; what it owns — and what this page edits — is each robot's revocation set. Revocations are grow-only and cannot be undone here.
