src/flashback/resim.rs, frame_not_held_reason: `<` replaced with `<=` in the
`sequence < first_sequence` guard. Equivalent for every gap a production path
builds: each comes from `first_frame_gap`, which reports a frame only when its
writer's held sequences do not contain it, and `first_sequence` is the lowest
held sequence, so the two are never equal.
